# Dead Signal - Complete Design & Implementation Plan

## Overview

Dead Signal is a long-form endgame quest chain designed around mystery, exploration, preparation, survival, and server-wide events.

The story begins with an unexplained radio transmission and ultimately reveals the true origin of the infection and apocalypse.

---

# Core Narrative

Players discover a repeating signal being transmitted during Dead Signal events.

What initially appears to be a scientific investigation gradually reveals:

- Ancient ruins high in the mountains.
- A long-forgotten containment system.
- Scientists attempting to understand the phenomenon.
- The true reason behind the infected.
- A Stability Machine that keeps something contained.
- A Rift leading to the ultimate source.

---

# Stage-By-Stage Implementation Plan

## Stage 1 - The Broadcast

### Trigger

DF Event Manager starts Dead Signal.

### System Actions

- Select random quest bunker.
- Generate bunker PIN.
- Activate bunker.
- Spawn lore crate.
- Start Morse broadcast.

### Broadcast Format

DS
<Grid>
<PIN>

# Apocalypse & Infection Lore

## Ancient Civilisation

Long before modern humanity, an unknown civilisation discovered the signal.

They built the mountain ruins and a containment system designed to suppress it.

---

## The Signal

The signal is not a conventional radio transmission.

It is a biological and cognitive phenomenon.

Exposure causes:

- Aggression
- Paranoia
- Violence
- Loss of identity
- Neurological degradation

---

## The Infected

The infected are not traditional zombies.

They are living people whose higher cognitive functions have been destroyed through prolonged exposure.

What remains:

- Instinct
- Fear
- Aggression

What was lost:

- Memory
- Reason
- Identity

---

## The Scientists

Modern researchers detected the signal and attempted to understand it.

They eventually rediscovered fragments of the ancient containment system.

To study the phenomenon they rebuilt portions of the original machinery, including the Stability Machine.

---

## The Real Purpose Of The Stability Machine

The machine was never intended to study the signal.

Its purpose was containment.

The scientists misunderstood its function.

---

## The Rift

The Rift is not created by the machine.

The machine suppresses it.

By repairing and activating the system during Dead Signal, players effectively disable the final lock.

---

## Final Revelation

The infection was never the cause of the apocalypse.

The infection was the first symptom.

The Dead Signal is the true cause.

The final document suggests the signal originates from somewhere beyond Earth and has been transmitting for thousands of years.

# Design Goals

- Long-term quest progression.
- Multiple play sessions required.
- Meaningful exploration.
- Strong lore payoff.
- Server-wide mystery.
- Integration with Dead Signal events.
- Competition between players.
- No tiered rewards.
- Equal quest locations.
- First player/group to progress gains an advantage.
